2. How many times does the program resize?

The program resizes 3 times, once when it tries the second push to make the size double
and be size 2. Then on the third push it doubles to become capacity 4. Then on the 6th push after a pop the 6th is the fifth item in the stack so it resizes again to make the size of the stack 8.

3. Time Complexity?

The time complexity is O(n) because of the resizing loop that iterates through the arrays.
